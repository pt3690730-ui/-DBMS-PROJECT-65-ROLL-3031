Cinema Screening and Ticket Booking System

Design and implementation of a database management system that schedules shows, sells seats and handles cancellations and refunds without ever double-booking.

01 · Problem statement

Why a cinema needs a proper database

The problem

Cinemas run many screens, shows and customers at once. Spreadsheets and ad hoc code let two people buy the same seat, schedule two films on one screen at the same time, or refund more than was paid.

The goal

One relational database that stores movies, screens, seats, shows, bookings, payments, snacks, cancellations and refunds, and enforces the rules itself.

Who uses it

  • Customers booking seats and snacks
  • Box-office staff
  • Managers reading revenue and occupancy reports

03 · Parts

Five groups of tables

Venue

cinema, screen, seat. Each screen has a fixed seat layout.

Programme

movie, screening. A screening is one movie on one screen at one time.

Sales

customer, booking, booked_seat, ticket, payment.

Snacks

concession_item, concession_order, order_item with limited stock.

After-sales

cancellation_policy, cancellation, refund.

04 · How it works

From booking to refund

Choose show→Pick seats→Add snacks→Pay→Tickets issued→Cancel (optional)→Refund by policy

A booking is one transaction: it commits as a whole or rolls back, so data is never half-saved.

06 · Design quality

Clean, safe and fast

3NF

15 of 17 tables are in strict third normal form. Two keep a documented, deliberate redundancy: screening end time and the screening id in booked seats.

Constraints

Primary and foreign keys, UNIQUE, NOT NULL, CHECK and DEFAULT.

Indexes

Movie title, show time, bookings and payments.

Transactions

Commit as a whole or roll back.
